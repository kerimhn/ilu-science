Arctic Research Starts Here

Based in Ilulissat, we support international scientists with logistics, transport, and field operations in one of the most accessible glacier systems in Greenland.

Research

Support for glaciology, oceanography, climate, and field-based science.

Logistics

Transport, accommodation, and coordination for Arctic field campaigns.

Infrastructure

Access to local networks, equipment, and operational expertise.

Why Ilulissat?

Ilulissat provides direct access to the UNESCO Icefjord, active glaciers, marine ecosystems, and well-developed logistics. It is one of Greenland’s most strategic hubs for Arctic science.
